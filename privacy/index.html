1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how DC Companies LLC (“DC Companies,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you visit dccompanies.net, email us, or join our informational text program. We are a family-owned, veteran-owned company based in Folsom, Louisiana.

This policy applies to DC Companies LLC only. Our text program is informational and customer-care only. It is not a marketing program.

2. Information we collect

We may collect:

  • Contact details you send us, such as name, email address, phone number, and message content.
  • Mobile number, opt-in keyword (START), confirmation reply (Y), opt-out (STOP), and help (HELP) messages for the SMS program.
  • Basic website technical data such as browser type, referring page, and approximate location derived from IP address, if our host or analytics provider records it.

We do not require an account to browse this site.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and operate our business units.
  • Send informational and customer-care texts after you opt in (see below).
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the website.
  • Meet legal, tax, and recordkeeping obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use the SMS program for marketing or advertising.

4. SMS and mobile information

If you text START to 844-526-4892 and reply Y, you opt in to informational texts from DC Companies LLC. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help. Full program terms are in our Terms of Service and on SMS Opt-In.

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile number, messaging opt-in data, or consent to third parties, affiliates, lead generators, or anyone else for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile numbers are used only to operate this informational text program and to respond to you.

Our messaging vendor (for example, the carrier network and the communications platform we use to send and receive texts) may process messages solely to deliver the service. That processing is not a sale or a marketing share of opt-in data.

5. Sharing

We may share information only as needed to:

  • Deliver email or website hosting, when a service provider processes data for us under our direction.
  • Comply with law, legal process, or a valid government request.
  • Protect the rights, safety, or property of DC Companies LLC, our users, or others.

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

6. Cookies and analytics

This is a static site. We do not currently run advertising cookies or sell browsing data. If a host or future analytics tool sets a cookie, it would be for basic traffic measurement, not for selling your data.

7. Retention

We keep inquiry and SMS records as long as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, and meet legal requirements. If you opt out by texting STOP, we retain the minimum record needed to honor that opt-out and avoid messaging you again unless you later opt in.

8. Your choices

  • Email us at brent@dccompanies.net to ask about, correct, or delete information we hold, subject to legal limits.
  • Text STOP to 844-526-4892 to leave the SMS program.
  • Text HELP to 844-526-4892 for SMS help.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights. Contact us to exercise them.

9. Children

This site and the SMS program are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Material changes will be posted on this page.
