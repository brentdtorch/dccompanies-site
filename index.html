About

A family business, built on discipline.

DC Companies LLC is a Louisiana-based, family-owned and U.S. Army combat veteran-owned company. Founded by an industrial construction executive after years running specialty welding and turnaround operations for major oil, gas, and chemical clients, it grew into a group of business units the family builds and runs together.

The standard comes straight from the field — and from the Army: clear orders, verified execution, and nothing left unaccounted for. That's not a tagline. It's how every unit under DC Companies operates.
